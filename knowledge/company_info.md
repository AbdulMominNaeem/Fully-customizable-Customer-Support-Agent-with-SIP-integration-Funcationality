## Booking and delivery

- Customers can book at any MG dealership or online through "Book My MG"
  on mgmotors.com.pk.
- Typical delivery times: MG4 and Cyberster about 3 months,
  MG5 EV about 4 months, U9 about 90 to 100 days.
  For other models, the dealer confirms the delivery time.
- The booking amount, payment method, and required documents are confirmed
  by the dealer. Usually a CNIC copy is needed for registration.

## Financing

- MG offers bank financing through partner banks. Customers can apply
  through the Financing page on mgmotors.com.pk or at any dealership.
- The partner bank list, down payment, markup rate, and tenure aren't
  published, so direct callers to a dealer or the UAN for exact terms.

## MG Exchange (trade-in and certified used cars)

- Trade in your current car at an MG dealer at fair market value and pay only
  the difference for a new MG.
- MG also sells certified used MG cars. Each one passes a 36-point inspection
  and comes with a 6 month or 10,000 km warranty (whichever comes first),
  covering parts and labour.

## Warranty

- ZS Hybrid+: 5 years or 150,000 km vehicle, 8 years or 150,000 km hybrid battery
- Binguo EV: 5 years or 100,000 km vehicle, 8 years or 150,000 km battery
- For other models, confirm the warranty with the dealer or the UAN.
- Warranty requires servicing at authorized MG dealerships on schedule.

## Service and maintenance

- Service can be booked on the call (the agent will log it),
  on mgmotors.com.pk (Schedule a Service), or at any MG dealership.
- Electric cars (MG4, Binguo, MG5, Cyberster) need much less maintenance:
  no engine oil, no oil filter. Brakes, tyres, AC filter, and coolant are
  still checked at service.
- Hybrids (ZS Hybrid+, HS Hybrid+) never need to be plugged in; the battery charges while driving.
- The plug-in hybrid (HS Super Hybrid) can be charged at home on AC,
  but can also run on petrol like a normal car if it isn't charged.
